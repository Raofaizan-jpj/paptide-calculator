Mission & Disclosures

About Peptide Calculator

Peptide Calculator was built to bring transparency, mathematical precision, and clarity to chemical concentration and dilution calculations without marketing hype or medical presumption.

Website Purpose

Calculations involving microgram-to-milligram conversions, volumetric dilutions, and syringe tick markings are mathematically straightforward, yet easy to miscalculate when done manually. Our purpose is to provide clean, accessible educational software where users can inspect formulas, normalize units, and verify arithmetic models in a safe sandbox.

What This Tool Does

  • Normalizes mass (g, mg, mcg) and liquid volume (L, mL) units using rigorous dimensional analysis.
  • Calculates exact solute concentration based on numbers provided by the user.
  • Computes the mathematical liquid volume in mL and syringe units corresponding to a user-entered dose.
  • Visualizes draw levels on simulated syringe barrels to help users understand tick relationships.

What This Tool Does NOT Do

Critical Boundary: This website is an educational arithmetic calculator. It is NOT a medical clinic, treatment advisor, or prescription tool.
  • It does NOT diagnose illnesses, health symptoms, or bodily conditions.
  • It does NOT recommend peptides, therapies, protocols, or substances.
  • It does NOT calculate or recommend what dose any individual should take.
  • It does NOT replace licensed medical professionals, pharmacists, or official pharmaceutical labeling.

Any numeric dose entered into the calculator is treated solely as a theoretical mathematical parameter supplied by the user.

Privacy & Local Computation Guarantee

Your privacy is paramount. Unlike modern web apps that log inputs or require user registrations:

Zero Data Transmission

100% of calculations execute directly inside your browser via Vanilla JavaScript. No numbers, inputs, or session records are ever transmitted to a server.

Zero Tracking or Ads

This website does not load behavioral tracking scripts, third-party analytics pixels, advertising beacons, or invasive tracking cookies.

Zero Storage

We do not store your values in cookies, databases, or local storage. Once you refresh or close your browser tab, all calculated values vanish.

Always Verify With Professionals

Reconstituted solutions, sterile handling, diluent selection (such as bacteriostatic water vs. sterile water), and dosage requirements are critical clinical matters. Always consult licensed medical specialists and adhere strictly to official pharmaceutical packaging inserts.

Medical & Educational Disclaimer

This calculator is for educational and mathematical purposes only. It does not provide medical advice, treatment recommendations, or personalized dosing instructions. Always follow the instructions provided by a qualified healthcare professional and the product's official labeling.
