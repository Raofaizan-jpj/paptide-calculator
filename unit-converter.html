Metric Unit Conversion Reference

In peptide calculations, mass and volume follow standard SI (International System of Units) metric prefixes where each tier represents a factor of 1,000:

Grams to Milligrams

1 g = 1,000 mg
To convert g to mg, multiply by 1,000.
Example: 0.005 g = 5 mg.

Milligrams to Micrograms

1 mg = 1,000 mcg
To convert mg to mcg, multiply by 1,000.
Example: 2.5 mg = 2,500 mcg.

Micrograms to Milligrams

1 mcg = 0.001 mg
To convert mcg to mg, divide by 1,000.
Example: 250 mcg = 0.25 mg.

Liters to Milliliters

1 L = 1,000 mL
To convert L to mL, multiply by 1,000.
Example: 0.01 L = 10 mL.

Milliliters to Liters

1 mL = 0.001 L
To convert mL to L, divide by 1,000.
Example: 2 mL = 0.002 L.

Micrograms to Grams

1 g = 1,000,000 mcg
To convert mcg to g, divide by 1,000,000.
Example: 5,000 mcg = 0.005 g.
