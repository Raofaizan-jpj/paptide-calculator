Understanding the Reconstitution Formula

Peptide reconstitution arithmetic relies on standard mass-by-volume dimensional analysis. When a dry powder of mass $M$ is dissolved completely in liquid diluent of volume $V_{liquid}$, the resulting concentration $C$ is:

Formulas Applied:

Concentration (C) = Mass ÷ Volume
Required Volume (V) = Dose ÷ Concentration

Example: 10 mg dry mass dissolved into 2 mL bacteriostatic solution creates a 5 mg/mL concentration. An entered dose of 500 mcg (0.5 mg) requires: 0.5 mg ÷ 5 mg/mL = 0.1 mL (10 units on a standard U-100 syringe).
