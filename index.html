Worked Examples

Educational Mathematical Examples

These calculations illustrate pure arithmetic relationships and are not recommended medical doses.

Example 1: Concentration Calculation

Given: 10 mg of peptide in 2 mL of diluent.
Formula: Concentration = 10 mg ÷ 2 mL
Result: 5 mg/mL (equal to 5,000 mcg/mL).

Example 2: Volume from Desired Dose

Given: Concentration = 5 mg/mL; desired mathematical dose = 250 mcg (0.25 mg).
Formula: Volume = 0.25 mg ÷ 5 mg/mL
Result: 0.05 mL (5 units on a U-100 syringe).

Example 3: Unit Normalization

Given: Convert 1.5 mg into micrograms.
Formula: Micrograms = 1.5 mg × 1,000
Result: 1,500 mcg.
